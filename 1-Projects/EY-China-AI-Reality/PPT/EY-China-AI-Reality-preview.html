Enterprise Action: From Tool Trial to Business Redesign

A practical path for moving from scattered AI use cases to measurable business transformation.

1. Identify high-value use cases
Prioritize processes with clear pain points and usable data
2. Move from Copilot to Agent
From assistance to connecting tasks, systems and accountable owners
3. Redesign workflows, not just tasks
Rethink data flow, approval, cross-functional collaboration
4. Build enterprise context
Turn business data / rules / experience into AI-ready knowledge
5. Balance global standards with local practice
Global governance + China local experimentation in parallel
6. Establish AI coding governance
Clarify security, IP, testing, release rules
7. Measure business value
Track cycle / cost / quality / risk / revenue
8. Manage two kinds of risk
Manage both 'risk of using AI' and 'risk of moving too slowly'
🎯 The goal is not more AI tools. It is redesigning how value is created, governed, and scaled.

The environment is shaped by open models, rapid experimentation, manufacturing depth, and business integration.
Distinctive combination of advantages
China's distinctive feature is the combination of speed, scale, market integration, and business experimentation.
What matters is not only model capability, but the shorter loop from technology to real business use.
AI is moving beyond productivity tools
AI is moving from productivity tools into workflows, decisions, software development, and organizational design.
The real management question is no longer whether AI can help. It is what should change inside the enterprise.
